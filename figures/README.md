>
> **The J.2 → K transition is NINE replacements, not ten:**
> `K2N`, `T135K`, `S144N`, `N158D`, `I160K`, `Q173R`, `K189R`, `T328A`, `HA2:S49N`.
> Residue 145 is Serine in J.2, J.2.4 and K alike, and in the A/Aichi/2/1968 H3-numbering

>
> **Root cause.** The panel diffed a *stored* results table's `ref_aa` against a *live*
> reference FASTA. The `J.2_int` root was corrected at precursor codon 161 (`aat` → `agt`)
> on 15 Jun 2026 and the May tables were never rebuilt, so the 2 Jul 2026 render compared a
> stale J.2 against a current K and emitted both `S144N` and `N145S`. Nothing warned.
>
> **Fix.** `guides/composites/fig01.yaml` places the fine-tuned model (`ESM2_650M_HA80`)
> render (`data/frozen_panels/fig1bc_ranked_mutations_finetuned_esm2_ha80.pdf`), matching the
> Results text (HA2:S49N highest at rank 7, T328A lowest at rank 578); `output/fig01_composite.pdf`
> and `fig01_composite.png` carry exactly the canonical nine. The 2 Jul file is kept as
> `data/frozen_panels/fig1bc_ranked_mutations.pdf` **for provenance only — do not place it.**
>
> **Standing risk this exposed:** re-plotting an old table against newer roots can silently
> invent or erase replacements. Full account in `LINEAGE_DEFINITIONS.md` section 6.

> ### Masked marginals vs. unmasked (wild-type) marginals
>
> By design across the manuscript:
> - **Figure 3, Figure 4, and Supplementary Figure SB** all use **masked marginals** by default (`alpha_sweep_by_lineage_masked`, `replacement_scores_masked`, and `dms_scores_masked`). Masked marginals score each position by replacing it with the PLM mask token, preventing wild-type sequence leakage from inflating untuned model scores.
> - **Unmasked marginals** are restricted to **Figure 2** (preserving the published unmasked forward pass) and **Figure SO** (the supplementary figure explicitly quantifying the difference between masked and unmasked estimators across DMS and codon strata).

## Rules every script follows

* Colours come from `mutacc.style` by role (base, fine-tuned, accessibility, biochemistry,
  chance), never from a literal. Okabe-Ito palette, chosen because the previous pair failed a
  contrast and chroma check.
* No script sets its own rcParams, calls `savefig`, or imports seaborn.
* Titles name the metric exactly; a smaller tagline says in plain words what it asks.
* Every reference line is labelled. Alpha is never shown bare.
* A caption never claims more than the table behind it shows.

`tests/test_figure_rules.py` enforces the first three by reading the source.

## Column definitions

Each `<stem>_values.tsv` has one row per plotted point; its columns are named after what they
hold. For the shared ones: `mean`, `lo`, `hi` are the lineage mean and its 95% percentile
bootstrap interval; `rho` is a Spearman correlation; `n` is a count of replacements and
`n_sites` a count of sites.
