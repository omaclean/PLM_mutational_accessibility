Scope. Every numeric claim in the abstract, introduction, results, discussion, methods and supplementary captions was checked. Claims resting on external citations (influenza mortality, the $29 billion cost, ESM-2's own training-set clustering) are outside the repository and were not checked against it.

One caveat on provenance. Some method details — software versions, the SARS-CoV-2 genome composition — exist in the repo only inside Preprint notes, which is the repo's record of the manuscript's own text rather than an independent derivation. Where an independent artefact existed it was used instead (the De Maio round-trip through data/spectra/sars_cov_2.csv, the 2015-02-27 corpus date, the ranks printed in the figure PDF itself).
