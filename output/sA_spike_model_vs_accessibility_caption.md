**Figure SA. The Figure 2 test on a second virus: SARS-CoV-2 spike.** Mutational
accessibility is the probability that a nucleotide mutation process (here the spectrum for
this virus, from data/spectra), given the reference codon at a site, produces a particular
amino-acid replacement. The models are protein language models (PLMs): the base model as
released, and the same model fine-tuned on sarbecovirus spikes. 5 SARS-CoV-2 spike
lineages are used (Wuhan-Hu-1_Reference, JN.1, XEC, LP.8.1, NB.1.8.1). They are variants
of one protein and share almost every site, so they are near-duplicates, not independent
replicates.

**(A, B)** Every possible replacement at every site of Wuhan-Hu-1_Reference, for the base
model (A) and the fine-tune (B), in the colours used for those models in Figure 2. The
three bands are the replacements needing one, two and three nucleotide changes. Axes are
logarithmic. The correlation in the subtitle is the Spearman rank correlation (1 = same
ranking, 0 = unrelated) over all replacements, averaged over the lineages; the dots are
one lineage. It is partly produced by the genetic code, because replacements that need
more changes are both rarer under mutation and less likely under the model; panel C
removes that.

**(C)** The same correlation inside each group of replacements that need the same number
of nucleotide changes, where the code cannot drive it. "Any" pools all replacements.
Markers are means over the lineages. As in Figure 2D, each model is shown twice: the
hollow marker is the plain Spearman correlation and the filled marker is the partial
Spearman correlation with Grantham similarity held constant, with the arrow between them
and a 95% percentile bootstrap interval that resamples sites on the filled marker.
Grantham (a chemical distance, sign flipped so that higher = more similar) is the
chemistry control: it has no mutational information, and is drawn on the tick with its own
interval. Pooling all replacements, the correlation that survives the control is 0.25 of
0.30 for the base model and 0.66 of 0.68 for the fine-tune. With three changes the
controlled correlations are 0.04 for the base model (0.02 to 0.06), 0.28 for the fine-tune
(0.26 to 0.31); Grantham itself is -0.15.

The epoch curve of Figure 2C is not shown: the archive holds no per-epoch checkpoints of
this backbone. The scores were produced by the exploration archive's pipeline; they are
shipped as a table and are not regenerated by this repository.
