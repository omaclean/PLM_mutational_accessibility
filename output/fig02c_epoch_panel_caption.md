**Figure 2C. Correlation with mutational accessibility across fine-tuning epochs.**
Spearman rank correlation between the model's probability for each amino-acid replacement
and its mutational accessibility, for the model as released ("raw", 0.274) and after each
of 20 epochs of fine-tuning. It rises to 0.622 after the first epoch and then stays
between 0.634 and 0.660 (epoch 20: 0.651): almost all of the change happens in the first
epoch.

The values are means over three lineages and were read back from the published version of
this panel (to about 0.0005); the per-epoch table behind it no longer exists. The lineages
are scored at the same sites, so they are pseudo-replicates of one another and the mean
stands in for what a single lineage would give.
