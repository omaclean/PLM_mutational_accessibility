**Figure 1. Every replacement ranked, by the model and by mutational accessibility.** All
10,754 amino-acid replacements available on the J.2 backbone (566 sites x 19 alternative
residues), ordered from the highest value to the lowest on a logarithmic scale, with the
nine replacements that separate J.2 from subclade K marked and labelled with their rank.
**(B)** the fine-tuned model's probability: HA2:S49N is the highest at rank 7 (top 0.1%)
and T328A the lowest at rank 578 (top 5.4%). **(C)** mutational accessibility: Q173R is
the highest at rank 241 (top 2.2%) and I160K the lowest at rank 3294 (top 30.6%).

Accessibility depends only on the codon and the target residue, so it takes far fewer
distinct values than there are replacements and panel C's ranks are heavily tied. The
marked ranks are competition ranks -- a tie takes the first rank of its block -- and the
widest block holding one of the nine is 29 replacements deep, which is why two of them
share a rank. Panel B's ordering has no ties among the nine.
